## Edition 04 interaction boundary

The new character DOB form is a self-declaration, not verification. Source S01 informs the context of a requested birthdate; it does not verify this exact interactive sequence. S03 informs the distinction between an estimated age and document birthdate returned to the platform. The full sample image exposing a fictional name, the script's choice to read a birthdate, checkbox confirmation, processing animation and preset review reasons are explicitly fictional. No claim is made that an actual AI or biometric system runs in this exhibition.

Needed for a closer reconstruction: dated UK declaration prompts, consent and recipient notices, document-field handling, actual alternative methods, appeal options and any published waiting-time statements. Character conditions and displayed results cannot establish real success/failure rates.
